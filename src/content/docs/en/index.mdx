## How this course works

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="The model first, then the code" icon="rocket">
    Every topic starts from a model — the compiler, fine-grained reactivity, the store contract — so Svelte's magic becomes mechanism you understand.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Svelte 5, verified" icon="random">
    Written against Svelte 5 and checked against the official docs — runes (`$state`/`$derived`/`$effect`/`$props`), snippets (`{@render}`), and `onclick` events are covered as they exist today, not the Svelte 4 way. Verified against Svelte 5.57.x and SvelteKit 2.70.x (Node 24).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Design, not just syntax" icon="approve-check">
    Not "here's $effect" but "here's why you probably don't need it, and what to derive instead." You'll reason about reactivity and data flow with confidence.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diagrams that explain" icon="puzzle">
    The compile step, the reactivity graph, snippet rendering, and the SvelteKit load flow as theme-aware Mermaid diagrams. Bilingual (English / ไทย) with saved quiz progress.
  </Card>
